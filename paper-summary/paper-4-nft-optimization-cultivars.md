## 🎯 TẠI SAO BÀI NÀY QUAN TRỌNG?

Bài báo này chứng minh rằng:

1. **Thiết kế NFT system** ảnh hưởng lớn đến năng suất
2. **Lựa chọn cultivar** rất quan trọng (không phải "lettuce" là đủ)
3. **Yield stability** cao nhất khi kết hợp đúng system + cultivar
4. **Physiological indicators** (chlorophyll, NR) liên quan trực tiếp đến năng suất

Đây là bằng chứng khoa học cho việc:

- Database cần lưu **cultivar**, không chỉ "crop type"
- System configuration ảnh hưởng đến predicted yield
- Monitoring chlorophyll & NR có giá trị

## 🔬 EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN

### Location & Conditions

- Lima, Peru (La Molina University)
- Summer 2021
- 50% shade mesh for temperature control

### Environmental Data

- Global radiation: 644 MJ·m⁻²·month⁻¹
- Average PPFD: 522.89 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- Temperature: 31.5°C (max) / 15.6°C (min)
- Day/Night avg: 25.82°C / 20.43°C
- Relative humidity: 66.4%

### Growth Timeline

1. **Day 0:** Seed germination in sand substrate
2. **Day 8:** First transplant to 4cm pots
3. **Day 20:** Transfer to NFT systems
4. **Day 48:** Harvest (25 days in NFT)

### Factorial Design: 2×3

- **2 Cultivars:**
  - **Tropicana** (Johnny's Selected Seeds)
  - **Starfighter** (Johnny's Selected Seeds)
  - Both: Curly type, heat-tolerant
- **3 NFT Modules:**
  - **Module I:** 8 channels, horizontal layout
  - **Module II:** 13 channels, pyramidal layout
  - **Module III:** 10 channels, pyramidal layout

- **Total:** 6 treatments × 10 replications = 60 plants

## 📊 KEY RESULTS

### 1. Agronomic Performance

#### Root System

- **NO significant differences** between treatments
- Fresh & dry root biomass independent of cultivar/module
- Root length uniform across all treatments

#### Stem Biomass

- **Module II & III > Module I** ✅
- **Tropicana > Starfighter** (80% higher) ✅
- Interaction effect significant (p < 0.02)

#### Leaf Biomass

- **Fresh weight:** No significant differences
- **Dry weight:**
  - Tropicana (Module III) > Starfighter (Module I)
  - Mixed behavior between treatments

#### Head Biomass

**Fresh head weight (g/plant):**
| Treatment | Fresh Weight | Dry Weight |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Tropicana + Module III | **Highest** ✅ | **Highest** ✅ |
| Tropicana + Module II | High | High |
| Starfighter + Module II | Moderate-High | Moderate |
| Tropicana + Module I | Moderate | Moderate |
| Starfighter + Module III | Moderate | Moderate |
| Starfighter + Module I | **Lowest** | **Lowest** |

#### Plant Height

- **Tropicana + Module III:** Tallest (elongation response)
- **Module II:** Similar height for both cultivars
- **Module I:** Shortest plants
- **Shade avoidance:** Tropicana shows stronger response

---

### 2. YIELD (Most Important!)

**Fresh Head Yield (kg·m⁻²):**

| Cultivar        | Module I | Module II    | Module III   |
| --------------- | -------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| **Tropicana**   | 11.07    | **14.14** ✅ | **13.96** ✅ |
| **Starfighter** | 11.53    | **13.45**    | 12.89        |

**Key findings:**

- **Highest yield:** Tropicana + Module II (14.14 kg·m⁻²)
- **Second highest:** Tropicana + Module III (13.96 kg·m⁻²)
- **Third highest:** Starfighter + Module II (13.45 kg·m⁻²)
- Module I significantly lower than II & III
- Yield difference mainly due to:
  - Plant density (Module II has 43.3 plants/m²)
  - Individual plant biomass
  - Cultivar performance

**Comparison with literature:**

- Previous DFT studies: 2.74-3.38 kg·m⁻² (Rodríguez-Delfín 2001)
- This NFT study: **11-14 kg·m⁻²**
- **NFT efficiency >> DFT** (3-5× higher)

---

### 3. Physiological Indicators

#### Total Chlorophyll Content

- **Module II > Module I > Module III** ✅
- Independent of cultivar (p > 0.4)
- Starfighter: Module I > Module III
- Higher chlorophyll indicates better photosynthetic capacity

#### SPAD Index (Relative Chlorophyll)

- **NO significant differences** (p > 0.3)
- Less sensitive to subtle changes
- All treatments: healthy range

#### Nitrate Reductase (NR) Activity

**Enzyme activity (key nitrogen metabolism indicator):**

- **Tropicana + Module II:** **Highest** ✅
- **Starfighter + Module I:** High
- **Module III:** Similar for both cultivars
- Significant interaction effect (p < 0.05)

#### Correlation

- **Total Chlorophyll ↔ NR Activity**
- Pearson r = 0.548 (p < 0.01)
- Highly significant positive correlation
- Both related to nutrient assimilation efficiency

---

### 4. Light Monitoring (PPFD)

**Average PPFD by module:**

- Control (open air, noon): 943.67 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- **Module I:** 558 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹
- **Module II:** 580.76 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ ✅ (highest)
- **Module III:** 558.82 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹

**Key insight:**

- Module II slightly higher PPFD
- Better light distribution in pyramidal design
- Correlates with higher chlorophyll & NR activity

## 🎓 SCIENTIFIC DISCUSSION

### 1. Module Design Impact

**Module II (Pyramidal, 13 channels) advantages:**

- Highest plant density (43.3 plants/m²)
- Best light distribution
- Optimal architecture for photosynthetic efficiency
- Highest total chlorophyll & NR activity
- **Best yield: 14.14 kg·m⁻² with Tropicana**

**Module III (Pyramidal, 10 channels):**

- Good performance, especially with Tropicana
- Lower density than II but higher individual biomass
- **Second best: 13.96 kg·m⁻² with Tropicana**

**Module I (Horizontal, 8 channels):**

- Lowest density
- Less optimal light distribution
- Smallest plants
- Significantly lower yield

### 2. Cultivar Selection Critical

**Tropicana characteristics:**

- **"Shade avoidance" phenotype**
- Greater stem elongation under 50% shade
- Higher fresh & dry biomass
- Better adaptation to Modules II & III
- **Recommended for NFT**

**Starfighter characteristics:**

- **"Shade tolerance" phenotype**
- More compact growth
- Lower stem biomass (80% less than Tropicana)
- Increased leaf area response
- Moderate performance

**Important:** Same species (Lactuca sativa) but **different physiological responses** → cultivar data must be stored!

### 3. Physiological Efficiency

**High NR activity indicates:**

- Efficient nitrogen assimilation
- Lower risk of nitrate accumulation in leaves
- Better quality lettuce (food safety)
- Optimal metabolic state

**Chlorophyll-NR correlation explains:**

- Both respond to light and nitrogen availability
- Module II provides best conditions for both
- Integrated indicator of plant health

### 4. Light Management

**50% shade mesh strategy:**

- Reduces stress from high radiation
- Maintains PPFD in optimal range (400-600 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹)
- Literature suggests 250-300 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ also effective
- Module design affects light distribution under mesh
